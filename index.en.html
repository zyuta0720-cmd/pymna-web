© 2026 Zyutama / MIT License (full text below). The web edition contains only the DC analysis core; transient analysis, schematic generation and LTspice export are not included. Your netlist is never sent anywhere. All calculations run locally in your browser. Results are for reference only. Verify final design decisions by other means. The Monte Carlo random sequence differs from the desktop edition (NumPy), so the same seed will not give identical values. Worst-case limitation: instead of an exhaustive search, the worst combination is found from the sign of each component's sensitivity plus iterative flipping. In circuits where a voltage is not monotonic in a component value, the true worst case may be missed. Power-check limitation: Max power is evaluated only over the combinations that maximize / minimize each node voltage. For a resistor with neither end at GND, the combination that maximizes its terminal voltage may not be included, so the result can be underestimated (PASS may be optimistic). Verify resistors with small margins separately.
